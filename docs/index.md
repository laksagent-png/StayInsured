## Start here

StayInsured is for the agent who runs their own book and keeps it in a
spreadsheet today.

1. [Install it and create your book](/guide/install-and-first-run) — a password
   and an agency name, and you are running.
2. [Import your spreadsheet](/guide/import-your-book) — clients, policies and
   history, without retyping any of it.
3. [Work the renewals](/guide/renewals) — the list of who to call, in the order
   their cover stops.

The [guide](/guide/) covers every operation, one page each, illustrated from the
running app.

## What it costs

Nothing. StayInsured is free and open source, and there is no account to create.
Your data is yours, in a file you can copy, back up and move.
